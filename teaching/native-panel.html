Principles and Model Notes

This tool calls Miner.mine, submit, receive, setBlock and queryBlockAtHeight on the current native network. Teaching transactions are stored in Header.teachingTransactions. The merchant balance is computed by replaying UTXOs from the observer node. Account balance in the original statistics table shows mining rewards; Merchant balance here shows teaching payments.

Step mode delivers native Headers through a controlled queue along existing outgoingPeers for reproducible observation. Steps do not represent real time, and race propagation is modeled with weights. Equal-difficulty chains are selected by accumulated block count. Higher fees do not guarantee inclusion. Defenses use reduced hash concentration, confirmation waiting, conflict detection and receipt validation. A 50-step observation window cannot establish permanent safety.

Native data tools remain available while sampling is paused during teaching mode. Coins and identities are abstract teaching objects. The simulation has no connection to wallets, real assets or blockchain nodes.
